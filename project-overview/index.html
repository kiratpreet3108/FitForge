    Project Overview

    What FitForge Is

    A web-based decision-support platform that digitizes school fitness assessments, tracks student athletic performance over time, and uses an explainable ML model to recommend suitable sports and training focus areas — with mandatory coach review before any recommendation reaches a student or parent.

    One-line positioning: "FitForge takes raw, unorganized fitness data and forges it into clear, coach-approved sports guidance."

    The Problem

    Schools conduct sports activities, but student fitness and performance data is rarely recorded systematically or analyzed over time. As a result:

    • Students with real sports potential can go unnoticed.
    • Interested students don't receive personalized, data-backed guidance on which sport suits them or what to improve.
    • Progress isn't tracked consistently, so improvement (or decline) goes unmeasured.
    • Coaches lack a structured way to compare students across age groups, fitness levels, or improvement needs.

    Goals

    • Digitize fitness-test and sports-performance records per student.
    • Track individual improvement over successive assessments (every 4–8 weeks).
    • Predict a fitness category using an explainable ML model.
    • Recommend a suitable sport and improvement focus, always with a stated reason.
    • Require coach review/approval before any recommendation is acted on.
    • Generate clear, exportable reports for students, parents, coaches, and admins.

    Non-Goals

    • FitForge does not determine a student's future or talent on its own — the model is coach-support only.
    • FitForge does not replace certified PE curriculum, professional talent scouting, or medical evaluation.
    • FitForge does not collect or process real medical records — only a simple clearance/restriction status.
    • FitForge does not claim scientific certainty in its recommendations; outputs are guidance, not verdicts.

    Who Uses It

    Persona Role Access
    Student Views own fitness report, recommended sport, training plan, progress Read-only, own data
    Coach/PE Teacher Enters assessments, evaluates skills, reviews/approves or overrides AI recommendations Read/write, assigned students
    School Admin Manages students, coaches, sports catalog, and system-wide reports Full access, admin scope
    Parent Optionally views their child's progress and recommendations Read-only, own child's data

    Why This Is a Strong Software Engineering Project

    It combines full-stack development, relational database design, applied machine learning, role-based security, data visualization, and reporting — all wrapped around a real, socially useful problem. It also forces genuine engineering discipline: coach-approval gating, explainability requirements, and honest handling of dataset limitations (see ML Model Plan) rather than presenting AI as an infallible black box.
